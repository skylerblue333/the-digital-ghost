# Family Easter Egg and Legacy Ledger

## Message to Luna, Summer, and Alexis

**Dad loves you. Dad believes in you. Dad is proud of you before you prove anything.**

The recurring code version is:

`PROUD_BEFORE_PROOF = true`

## Motifs

- **Luna / chess knight** — thinking, patience, choices, and permission not to see the whole board.
- **Summer / rain** — storms pass; difficult seasons do not define a person.
- **Alexis / seashell** — small details and ordinary memories can carry enormous meaning.
- **Orange morning light / all three** — Dad would choose ordinary family time over dramatic answers.
- **Christmas self-moving chessboard** — Skyler imagines digging plants for two or three days to afford an expensive board whose pieces move like movie magic, hoping one daughter becomes curious about chess, her sisters follow, Kaylee helps preserve the board, and Alexis eventually gets her turn.

The expensive board is not a demand for achievement. It is an invitation to sit at the same table.

## Editorial rule

These family signals are not surveillance evidence, prophecy, or supernatural communication. The daughters remain independent people, not plot leverage. Future edits should preserve at least one Luna/chess beat, one Summer/rain beat, one Alexis/seashell beat, the phrase **proud before proof**, and the Christmas chess-table memory.
