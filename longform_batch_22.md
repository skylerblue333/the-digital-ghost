# Long-Form Expansion Batch Twenty-Two

## Chapter Fifty-Seven — The Name on the Wall

The reprimand stayed on the wall for thirty days.

Skyler passed it each morning. Visitors read it while waiting for appointments. Some recognized his name. Some did not. A few stared at him and then at the document, trying to match the person to the record.

The old version of him would have removed it after the first week.

The new version learned that accountability was not a performance if no one could see it.

At home, the girls had questions.

“Did you get in trouble?” Summer asked.

“Yes.”

“Are you bad?”

“No.”

“How do you know?”

“I don’t always.”

The answer made them quiet.

Hanna sat beside him at the kitchen table. “You can tell them that being wrong is not the same as being bad.”

“It can become bad if you refuse to correct it.”

Luna looked at the blank card. “Then the card means you can change the rule.”

“Sometimes.”

“Who decides?”

“Everyone affected, if possible.”

Alexis frowned. “That takes too long.”

“Yes.”

“Then why do it?”

Skyler looked at the girls. Their faces were not symbols. They were not proof of his redemption. They were people waiting for an answer that would shape how they treated mistakes.

“Because fast rules can hurt people before anyone has time to stop them.”

The girls considered this.

Then Summer placed a strawberry on his plate.

“Eat,” she said.

The house returned to its ordinary rhythm.

That night, Skyler removed his name from a draft statement that described him as the founder of Open Door. The project was not his alone. The name on the wall had taught him that recognition could become another kind of control.

He signed the next document as **one member of the rotating council**.

The title looked smaller.

It also left room for other people to enter.
