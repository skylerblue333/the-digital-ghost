**Genres:** Literary fiction; psychological drama; speculative fiction; technology thriller.

**Short description:** *Loathing in the Woes of Ruthlessness: The Chosen One* is a psychological techno-thriller about evidence, consent, identity, power, and the cost of surviving a world that wants to turn pain into destiny.

**Long description:** Skyler Blue Spillers’s *Loathing in the Woes of Ruthlessness: The Chosen One* follows Skyler through a fictional world of decentralized influence, damaged records, digital identity, institutional pressure, and the emotional aftermath of being treated as a symbol. As the protagonist searches for the truth behind a system of manipulation, the story questions whether every pattern has a single author, whether technology can protect people without controlling them, and whether ruthlessness can remain a tool without becoming an identity. The novel’s central conflict is not simply power against power. It is power against humanity, certainty against evidence, and survival against the temptation to become the darkness one is fighting.

## Search visibility limits

No one can guarantee a search-engine ranking or immediate indexing. The strongest legitimate approach is consistent public naming, a dedicated page with unique text, accurate metadata, links from the author website and GitHub profile, an eventual Amazon Author Central profile, and submission of the owned website sitemap through Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
