## Required Before Clicking Publish

Complete a professional copyedit. Confirm the public author name, copyright holder, publication year, and final description. Open the EPUB in KDP’s Online Previewer and inspect phone, tablet, and Kindle-reader views. Confirm that the table of contents, chapter headings, scene breaks, italics, and page transitions are correct.

For paperback, confirm the actual page count after the final interior PDF is locked. Use Amazon’s Cover Calculator to generate a full-wrap template. Combine the back artwork, calculated spine, front artwork, final back-cover text, and barcode-safe area into one PDF. Do not upload the separate front and back PNG files as a paperback cover. Order a proof copy and inspect it before enabling the paperback for sale.

## Fictionalization and Rights Check

The manuscript must remain clearly presented as fiction. Before submission, remove any real private contact details and verify that no real person, government, agency, ethnic group, or public figure is presented as secretly responsible for fictional criminal conduct without transformation and narrative framing. Confirm that all song fragments are original fictional text and that the author owns the rights to every image and written element uploaded.
