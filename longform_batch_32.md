# Long-Form Expansion Batch Thirty-Two

## Chapter Sixty-Eight — The Rule at Home

Hanna wrote the household rule on the refrigerator.

**NO INVESTIGATION AFTER NINE.**

Skyler read it while making coffee.

“That is not enforceable,” he said.

“It is not a law. It is a boundary.”

“What if something happens?”

“Then we decide whether it is urgent.”

“Who decides?”

“We do.”

He looked at the rule again.

The project had rules for access, correction, and refusal. His home had survived on instinct. The Society had entered through that gap.

At nine that night, a message arrived showing a photograph of the library’s back entrance.

Skyler wanted to open the file.

Hanna reached for his hand.

“What is urgent?”

He looked at the photograph’s preview. The image could be a threat. It could be a public street. It could be an old photograph reposted to create fear.

“I don’t know.”

“Then it waits until morning.”

The decision felt impossible for three minutes.

At 9:04, he placed the phone in the ceramic bowl.

At 9:17, the message was still there.

At 9:32, he checked the girls’ rooms. They were asleep.

At 10:01, he began to breathe normally.

In the morning, Mara’s replacement at the library confirmed that the photograph showed the entrance from two years earlier. The angle was public. The account had posted it beside a false claim about a break-in.

Nothing had happened during the night.

Something had happened to Skyler’s fear.

He told Hanna.

She nodded. “The rule worked.”

“It was coincidence.”

“Maybe.”

He smiled. “You sound like Mara.”

“No. I sound like someone who wants to sleep.”

They laughed in the kitchen.

The laugh mattered because it did not solve anything.

It made room for another day.
